Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Indexed Pages Finder shows which pages of your websites Google has indexed, using your own Google Search Console data. It has no servers of its own; everything runs in your browser.

Google account access

  • You sign in with Google through Chrome's built-in sign-in.
  • The extension requests read-only Search Console access (webmasters.readonly). It can't change anything in your Search Console account or on your website.
  • Your sign-in token is managed by Chrome and used only to call Google's Search Console APIs.
  • Sign out in the extension removes the token and revokes access.

Data stored on your device

  • The last site you selected and your inspection limit setting.
  • The results of your last scan (URLs, index status, clicks, impressions, position).

All of it is kept in Chrome's local storage on your device, and it's deleted when you sign out or uninstall the extension.

Data transmission

  • Requests go only to Google's Search Console APIs (www.googleapis.com, searchconsole.googleapis.com).
  • To read sitemaps, the extension fetches sitemap files from your own site, and only after you allow access to that site.
  • In Any site mode, the extension reads the public sitemaps of the domain you enter and asks the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) for archived URLs of that domain. No sign-in is used and nothing about you is sent.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Use of Google user data

Indexed Pages Finder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to show you your own indexing reports. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared.

Analytics and tracking

None.

Permissions

  • identity: sign in with Google.
  • storage: remember your settings and last results locally.
  • Optional site access: read the sitemaps of the site you scan, and web.archive.org for Any site mode; asked separately for each site.
