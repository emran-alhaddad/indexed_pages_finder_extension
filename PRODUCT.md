# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agencies and freelancers doing SEO and web work for clients. They open the popup in the middle of a task, usually a site audit or a migration: find out what Google has indexed, find pages that dropped out, and list every old URL of a client's site so redirects can be written in the new codebase. They juggle several client sites and want an answer in seconds, then an export.

## Product Purpose

A free Chrome extension that lists a site's pages and their index status. "My sites" mode uses the user's Google Search Console data: real indexed pages, clicks and the reason a page isn't indexed. "Any site" mode needs no account: it combines the site's sitemaps with the Wayback Machine. Exports go to CSV and JSON, or straight into redirect rules (nginx, Apache, Next.js, Laravel). Success means the user gets from "which URLs exist" to a file they can paste into a project in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

A precise instrument: exact, calm, quietly confident. It reads like a measuring tool, not a marketing page. Numbers and paths are the content and get the best typography. It uses plain words and makes no promises it can't keep.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS popups: gradient buttons, glass cards, big hero numbers with tiny labels.
- Rows of identical colored pill chips.
- Dark-navy "SEO tool" look with neon accents.
- Anything that looks like a template with the logo swapped.

## Design Principles

1. The data is the interface. Paths, counts and reasons get the prime space; chrome stays thin.
2. One glance, one decision. The coverage of a site should be readable in a single visual before any list.
3. Honest about uncertainty. Say where every URL came from (Search Console, sitemap, archive) and what is unknown.
4. Fast to the file. Export and redirect generation are first-class, not buried.
5. Familiar controls. Native selects, inputs and buttons that behave like the platform.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in both light and dark themes, a full keyboard path, visible focus, and status never shown by color alone (glyph plus label). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
